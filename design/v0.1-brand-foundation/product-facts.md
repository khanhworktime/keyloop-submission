# Product and brand facts

Verified: 2026-07-13

## Product context

- The repository product is an Intelligent Inventory Dashboard for dealership
  managers, focused on stock visibility, aging vehicles, manager actions,
  Zone/Slot location management, and vehicle master data.
- The design target is a responsive browser application: detailed on desktop,
  touch-efficient on tablet/iPad, and optimized for fast checking on mobile.

## Keyloop context

- Keyloop describes itself as an automotive technology company serving
  retailers and manufacturers, with an Experience-First mission.
- Its Fusion Automotive Retail Platform organizes capabilities into Demand,
  Supply, Ownership, and Operate domains.
- Keyloop publicly positions Vehicle Hub around stock visibility, margin, and
  control of a vehicle's journey, which closely overlaps this dashboard's
  inventory-management context.
- Official Keyloop marketing and distributor guideline PDFs exist. They can be
  used for logo and brand-rule extraction if this project should visually align
  with the real Keyloop brand.
