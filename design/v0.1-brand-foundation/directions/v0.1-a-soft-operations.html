01 · Identity

Clear space is part of the mark.

The official slate wordmark anchors light surfaces. It is never redrawn, shadowed, cropped, or separated; restraint protects recognition.

01

Keep clear space of at least half the logo height on every side.

02

Use the complete mark at a minimum digital width of 150px.

03

Use slate on light surfaces; do not add outlines, effects, or new colorways.

04

This exploration is Keyloop-aligned, not an official released product.

05 · Controls and semantics

Comfort is measurable.

Touch targets are a system constraint, not visual padding. Status semantics combine color and language, with visible focus and no hover dependency.

Target guidance

44px is the floor. Frequent tablet actions use 48–56px for reliable one-handed operation.

One language, three priorities.

Desktop preserves visibility. Tablet promotes touch. Mobile narrows the job to exceptions and immediate actions, using progressive disclosure instead of squeezing the table.
