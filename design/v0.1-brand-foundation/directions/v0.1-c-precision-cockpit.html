03 / SEMANTICS

Color confirms. Language explains.

Status is always paired with a label and geometric marker. Controls prioritize clear bounds, selected states, and visible keyboard focus.

Status language

Never color-only
Ready

Positive completion or availability. Green is paired with a square marker and explicit label.

Attention

Aging-stock attention or a manager decision. Coral remains purposeful, not decorative.

In transit

Informational movement state. Blue supports progress without implying completion.

Blocked

Action cannot continue. Error red is reserved for a concrete operational stop.

Action hierarchy

Target guidance

444856
