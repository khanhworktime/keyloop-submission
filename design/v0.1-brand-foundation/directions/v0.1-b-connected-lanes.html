Status is always redundant: colour, shape, and plain-language label travel together.

Status semantics

Signal meaning,
not decoration.

A status treatment should answer what state this is and what kind of attention it needs. Never ask colour to carry that message alone.

ReadyPositive / availableConfirmed state; no immediate intervention implied.
Aging attentionReview neededCoral is reserved for aging stock and high-attention feedback.
Pending moveIn progressWarn without suggesting failure; pair with next-step copy.
Data issueAction requiredError red denotes a failed or invalid state that needs correction.
