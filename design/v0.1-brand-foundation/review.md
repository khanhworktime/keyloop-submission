# Brand direction review

Status: reviewed; Direction C selected and refined in `v0.1-d`

Open `index.html` for the side-by-side gallery. Each direction uses the same
official Keyloop identity, English UI content, responsive contract, and
operational product model. The difference is the composition and emphasis.

## Direction A — Soft Operations

The balanced baseline. Airy mist surfaces, a floating slate sidebar, and clear
information groupings keep the experience calm without losing operational
detail. Best fit when implementation confidence and broad manager usability
are the priority.

## Direction B — Connected Lanes

The relational direction. A continuous lane makes Zone → Slot → Inventory
Record and Master → Unit relationships visible. Best fit when location and
movement are the product's strongest differentiators.

## Direction C — Precision Cockpit

The dense direction. A framed workspace, selected-record rail, and compact
table hierarchy expose more context on desktop while tablet and mobile retain
large, explicit controls. Best fit when expert operational scanning is the
priority.

## Recorded decision

Direction C is the primary language. Direction A contributes typography and
spacing discipline. Direction B contributes widget, popup, popover, and status
composition, with semantic colors corrected for meaning and contrast. The
result is documented in `../v0.1-d-precision-guideline/brand-guideline.md`.

## Promotion gate

The selected Precision direction will be promoted to
`v0.2-component-language`, where navigation, buttons, filters, tables, status,
drawers, sheets, Zone/Slot controls, and responsive behavior become reusable
component specifications.
