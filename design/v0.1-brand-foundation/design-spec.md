# Brand foundation visual exploration specification

## Product and audience

The Intelligent Inventory Dashboard is a responsive operational product for
dealership managers. Its job is to make inventory status, aging stock,
manager actions, vehicle locations, and master data understandable at a glance
without flattening the detail required for confident decisions. The work is a
submission for Keyloop and therefore uses the official Keyloop logo, palette,
typography direction, and Experience-First character. All prototype copy is in
English.

## Output

Create three self-contained HTML brand-guideline explorations under
`design/v0.1-brand-foundation/directions/`, with a shared 1440px desktop review
canvas that remains usable down to tablet width. Each exploration must show the
same real design inputs: official logo, palette, typography, floating sidebar,
buttons, data row, status treatment, touch target guidance, and one compact
mobile specimen. No invented production statistics are needed; labels can use
clearly marked representative content from the accepted product contract.

The three directions must differ structurally, not merely by color. Direction
A should be the most balanced and implementation-ready. Direction B should
emphasize connected information and spatial relationships. Direction C should
push operational density while preserving softness and touch comfort. Each
direction is a review artifact, not production React code.

## Core content

- Official Keyloop identity and logo-use rules.
- UI color hierarchy and accessible status semantics.
- Plus Jakarta Sans headings and IBM Plex Sans UI typography.
- Floating navigation model for Overview, Inventory, Locations, Activity, and
  Master Data.
- Responsive behavior: detailed desktop, fast tablet interaction, fast-check
  mobile hierarchy.
- Touch-first component qualities: large targets, clear selected states,
  bottom/action sheets, and no hover dependency.
- Softness through surface tone and spacing while retaining professional data
  density and clear boundaries.

## Constraints

Do not create the eight final screens during this brand-foundation milestone.
Do not introduce additional brand imagery, vehicle photography, or unofficial
logo variants. Do not use decorative gradients, emoji, generic AI visuals, or
meaningless metric cards. Status colors must be paired with labels or symbols.
The official logo must remain intact and have clear space.

## Visual source and motif

The form comes from the Keyloop loop: inventory records connect to vehicle
masters, Slots connect to Zones, and actions connect to history. This becomes a
soft continuous rail, nested relationship map, or uninterrupted edge treatment
depending on the direction. The second motif is the dealership lane: orderly,
repeatable bays that can structure dense information without turning into a
literal illustration.

## Review criteria

The chosen direction must feel recognizably Keyloop-aligned, support dense
desktop workflows, remain comfortable at tablet scale, and reduce to a clear
mobile exception-checking experience. It must pass WCAG-oriented contrast and
touch sizing checks before being promoted to `v0.2-component-language`.
