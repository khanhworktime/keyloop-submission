# v0.2.4 Overlays & Decisions review

Status: awaiting manager Keep/Fix review

## Assumptions

- Managers should retain record context while making a decision.
- Interruption weight should match consequence, not visual novelty.
- Tablet and mobile decision surfaces must favor reachability over density.

## Review questions

- Does each overlay feel proportionate to the decision it contains?
- Is the difference between a modal task and destructive confirmation obvious?
- Does the record remain understandable without competing with the overlay?
- Are safe and destructive actions ordered clearly?
- Do tablet drawer and mobile sheet transformations feel comfortable for touch?

## Promotion gate

Keep this family at early review. Screen composition begins after manager
feedback on interruption weight, safety copy, action order, and responsive form.
